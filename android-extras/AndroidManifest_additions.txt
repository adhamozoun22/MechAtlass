Add these lines to android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml

1) next to the existing <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" /> line (outside <application>):

    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS" />

2) inside <application ...> ... </application> (for example right after the opening <application> tag):

    <meta-data android:name="com.google.firebase.messaging.default_notification_icon"
               android:resource="@drawable/ic_stat_mechatlas" />
    <meta-data android:name="com.google.firebase.messaging.default_notification_color"
               android:resource="@color/colorPrimary" />

(the second meta-data is optional; if the build says @color/colorPrimary is missing, delete that block or use android:value="#0284C7" instead of android:resource)
